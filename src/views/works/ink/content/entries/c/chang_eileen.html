Eileen Chang 張愛玲

Eileen Chang 張愛玲 — aka Zhang Ailing, born Zhang Ying 張煐1920-1995 — was a novelist, screenwriter and essayist, and a major figure in 20th century Chinese literature.

Many of Chang's works focus on the war-torn 1940s of Republican era 中華民國1912-1949 Shanghai and colonial Hong Kong, and her life in those cities under and post-occupation has taken on a romanticised vision of that time. She is perhaps best known overseas today as the author of Lust, Caution色，戒, which Ang Lee 李安 adapted for the screen in 2007. This and other novellas written in the latter part of her career — such as the post-humously published (and as-yet untranslated) 同學少年都不賤 — show her talent for depicting characters with complex inner lives and the social tensions that exist between them.

In addition to her fiction she was an active translator, and her research on Cao Xueqin's 曹雪芹1715-1763 Dream of the Red Chamber紅樓夢 is credited with having revived scholarly interest in the classic novel.
