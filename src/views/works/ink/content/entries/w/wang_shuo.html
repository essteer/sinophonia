Wang Shuo 王朔

Wang Shuo 王朔1958 is a novelist and screenwriter. Known for his liu mang 流氓 or hooligan style of writing, his works have been popular source material for film and television since the late 1980s.

The dark humour of Please Don't Call Me Human千萬別把我當人 gets close to the bone through the exploration of a useful idiot willing to do whatever is asked of him on the pretext of restoring national honour.

This is amongst the funniest writing I've read, on the level of Joseph Heller's Catch 22 and at times worthy of Monty Python. Wang has a knack for honing in on and relentlessly prodding social absurdities in his satire — prompting one Midnight Talks 大内密談 host to lovingly refer to him as "that bastard" （“那個混蛋”）. Published in the 1980s, a similar work could not evade censorship today.

Wang's output includes several screenplays that have been adapted for film and television, including the absurdist The Troubleshooters 頑主 directed by Mi Jiashan 米家山, which follows three founders of a Beijing firm that promises to resolve any problem their clients face.
