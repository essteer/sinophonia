Eye on East Asia 東亞觀察局

Eye on East Asia東亞觀察局 is a current affairs podcast on China, Japan and Korea, with a focus on modern politics within the context their shared cultural and historical ties.

Journalists Fan Yiru 梵一如, Shao Qingqing 少青青 and Quan Xiaoxing 權小星 hold lively discussions on developments involving these countries and the ever fraught state of relations between their respective governments and people.

Domestic political analysis cuts much deeper on Japan and Korea, but the hosts are skilled communicators and engaging even when delving into potentially opaque subjects such as electoral systems and intraparty rivalries. The show takes on much more than government organs and grim geopolitics though, often picking up notable topics from everyday culture and online goings-on.

For those interested in the politics and culture of East Asia, this series is a great resource for sustained coverage of ongoing events, as well as insight into conversations taking place within the PRC about the tense diplomacy of the region.
