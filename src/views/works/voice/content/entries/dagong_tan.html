Dagong Tan 打工談

Over 46 episodes released between 2020 and 2023, the hosts of Dagong Tan打工談 — 'Labour Talks' — interview working-class people in Guangdong province on how they make their living, the social status of rural and blue-collar workers, and the struggles they face in their work and personal lives.

Most episodes are conversations with a single guest about their circumstances, often highlighting difficulties that arise from a lack of labour rights and minimal options to contest workplace disputes; while others are more thematic and feature multiple speakers in a panel format.

The hosts' have a relaxed interview style that gives their guests room to represent themselves with dignity, contrary to prevailing stereotypes of the so-called 'low-end population' 低‍端人‍口 that sees little benefit from the economic growth it has fuelled.

Mainstream discourse on this core social segment is so often limited to vapid propaganda, or at best the dehumanising top-down perspective of macroeconomic analysis. Dagong Tan offers a rare glimpse of the reality of labour in the People's Republic in the workers' own words.
