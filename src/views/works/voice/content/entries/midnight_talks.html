Midnight Talks 大内密談

A long-running entertainment series with over 1,300 episodes since its launch in 2013 — and still strong going in 2026 — Midnight Talks大内密談 is a high-energy pop culture talk-show led by charismatic main host Xiang Zheng 相征.

The show has several supporting hosts on rotation with regular guests from the music and entertainment industries, and one-off feature episodes that touch on most things under the sun and above the red lines.

The production quality is consistently excellent, the hosts really know their music and are for the most part very funny to listen to, which makes this a reliable option. My start with Chinese roughly coincided with the show's launch, and the general energy kept me happily tuned in from when I knew just a few words and on through fluency.
