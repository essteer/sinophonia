CyberPink 疲憊嬌娃

CyberPink 疲憊嬌娃 is a pop-culture podcast hosted by four Chinese-American and/or US-based Chinese women who explore mainstream films, novels, video games and social events as backdrops for discussions on identity, sexism and politics.

The subjects of the episodes sometimes appear trivial at face-value (an episode of a hit TV show, or a celebrity feud), but the hosts' conversations are reliably interesting and draw connections to broader questions such as around identity politics in the east and west, and experiences of intergenerational conflicts within migrant families.
