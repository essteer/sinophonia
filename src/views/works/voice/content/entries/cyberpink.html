CyberPink 疲憊嬌娃

CyberPink 疲憊嬌娃 is a pop-culture podcast hosted by four Chinese-American women who are journalists for US media outlets. The show explores mainstream films, novels, video games and news as backdrops for discussions on identity, sexism and politics.

The conversations are reliably interesting — even on inane subjects like reality TV and celebrity feuds — and draw connections to broader questions such as around identity politics in the east and west, and experiences of intergenerational conflicts within migrant families.

A recurring theme is the hosts' unease at the need to cater to foreign audiences' preconceptions in their reporting on China, in lieu of a more nuanced discourse that reflects their ambivalence about the two cultures they inhabit. Given the surface-level nature of typical media coverage on either side, the show is worth following for its more critical perspective.
